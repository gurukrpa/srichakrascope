## 1. WHO YOU ARE RECRUITING (Target Audience)

These are your 5 buyer personas. Each one has a different pain point — your ads must speak to all of them:

### Persona 1 — "The Frustrated Teacher" 🧑‍🏫
- **Who:** School teacher (5–15 years experience), B.Ed/M.A., aged 28–45
- **Pain:** Loves guiding students but has no formal credential, school doesn't take them seriously for counsellor role
- **Dream:** Become the school counsellor, add ₹15,000+ to salary
- **Trigger Message:** *"You've been guiding students for years without a certificate. Change that in 6 weeks."*

### Persona 2 — "The Psychology Graduate" 🎓
- **Who:** Fresh B.Sc/M.Sc Psychology graduate, 21–27 years, looking for a niche
- **Pain:** Degree is broad, no specific career entry point, clinical path is expensive/competitive
- **Dream:** Be a certified expert who gets paid consulting school students
- **Trigger Message:** *"Your psychology degree + our certification = a career in school counselling within 90 days."*

### Persona 3 — "The Ambitious Parent" 👩‍👧
- **Who:** Stay-at-home parent or part-time worker, 30–42, strongly involved in child's education
- **Pain:** Helped own child through career confusion, realised no one does this professionally
- **Dream:** Start a home-based or school-visiting counselling practice
- **Trigger Message:** *"You guided your child's career. Now get certified to guide 100 others — on your own schedule."*

### Persona 4 — "The HR / Corporate Switcher" 💼
- **Who:** HR professional or corporate trainer, 28–40, wants meaningful work
- **Pain:** Feels corporate work is hollow, wants to work with youth, needs a credential to pivot
- **Dream:** Run workshops in schools, build consulting income on the side
- **Trigger Message:** *"Your HR experience + career counselling certification = a business that changes lives."*

### Persona 5 — "The School Management Buyer" 🏫
- **Who:** Principal, school owner, or education trust manager
- **Pain:** CBSE/State board mandates counsellors; hiring externally is expensive and unreliable
- **Dream:** Train an in-house teacher to be a certified school counsellor
- **Trigger Message:** *"Train your own teacher. Create your school's certified career counsellor in 6 weeks."*

## 2. PLATFORM STRATEGY

### Primary Platforms (Do These First)

#### A. WhatsApp Broadcast + Status — FREE, Highest Conversion
- **Use:** Directly message parents, teachers, educators in existing network
- **Action:** Create a dedicated broadcast list of 200+ contacts (alumni parents, past event attendees, school contacts)
- **Status Campaign:** 5-frame status series (same style as SCOPE campaign)
- **Conversion rate:** 5–8% direct from warm WhatsApp list

#### B. Instagram + Facebook Ads — Paid, Best for Reach
- **Platform split:** 60% Instagram (Reels + Stories), 40% Facebook (Feed + Groups)
- **Why Instagram:** Reaches teachers, young graduates, aspirational parents aged 25–42
- **Why Facebook:** Reaches school groups, parent communities, education forums
- **Ad Formats (in order of priority):**
  1. Reels (15–30 sec) — highest reach, lowest cost
  2. Story Ads — direct CTA to WhatsApp
  3. Carousel — show curriculum, benefits, testimonials
  4. Lead Generation Ad — collect name + phone directly in Facebook

#### C. LinkedIn Ads (For Persona 4 — HR/Corporate)
- **Target:** HR professionals, corporate trainers, L&D managers, Tamil Nadu / South India
- **Format:** Single image + Lead Gen form
- **Budget:** Smaller portion, but higher-quality leads
- **CPC:** ₹80–₹150 per click but leads convert better

#### D. YouTube Shorts / Ads — Awareness & Credibility
- **Use:** The existing Dronacharya-style video adapted for counsellors
- **Format:** 15-sec unskippable pre-roll on education/parenting content
- **Targeting:** Parents watching school board videos, teachers watching pedagogy content

### Secondary / Free Channels (Do These in Parallel)

| Channel | Action | Expected Outcome |
|---------|--------|-----------------|
| **Facebook Groups** | Post in "Teachers of Tamil Nadu", "CBSE Parent Network", "Education Entrepreneurs India" | 5–10 leads per post |
| **LinkedIn Posts** | Weekly posts on career counselling need in India — no direct selling | Build credibility, 2–5 leads/week |
| **Telegram** | Pin in existing Srichakra groups; create a free "Career Counselling Insights" channel | Nurture → convert |
| **Google My Business** | Post the program + WhatsApp link on GMB | Free local visibility |
| **Email** | 3-email drip to existing SCOPE customer database | 3–5 warm re-engagements |
| **School Visits / Webinar** | 1-hour free online webinar: "How to become a school career counsellor" | 15–20 registrations, 3–5 direct converts |
