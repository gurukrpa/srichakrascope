🧠 Psychology & Behavioural Sciences

India's mental-health awareness boom is creating massive demand for trained psychologists, counsellors and behavioural specialists.

Active listeningEmpathyResearch methodologyStatistics (SPSS)Ethical practiceReport writing

🧠 Psychology & Behavioural Sciences

Clinical Psychologist

Diagnoses and treats mental health disorders — registered with the Rehabilitation Council of India (RCI).

High growth Traditional
QualificationsBA/B.Sc Psychology · MA/M.Sc Psychology · M.Phil Clinical Psychology (RCI-recognised)
Entrance ExamsCUET · NIMHANS entrance · University entrance exams
Top InstitutionsNIMHANS Bangalore · Christ University · TISS Mumbai · Delhi University · IIPR Bangalore
Eligible StreamsArts · Science · Commerce
Typical salary range (India) 4–15 LPA (private practice can be much higher)

Counselling Psychologist / School Counsellor

Helps individuals navigate life issues — career, relationships, anxiety, school stress.

High growth Traditional
QualificationsBA Psychology + MA Counselling Psychology · Diploma in Guidance & Counselling
Entrance ExamsCUET · University entrance · IGNOU PG Diploma
Top InstitutionsTISS Mumbai · Christ University · Banyan Academy Chennai · Jamia Millia Islamia
Eligible StreamsArts · Science · Commerce
Typical salary range (India) 3–10 LPA

Industrial-Organisational (I-O) Psychologist

Improves workplace productivity, hiring, employee engagement and leadership development for companies.

High growth Emerging
QualificationsMA Psychology (I-O specialisation) · MBA HR optional
Entrance ExamsCUET · TISSNET · XAT
Top InstitutionsTISS · XLRI Jamshedpur · IIPM · Christ University
Eligible StreamsArts · Commerce · Science
Typical salary range (India) 6–20 LPA

UX Researcher / Behavioural Designer

Studies user behaviour to design better digital products — used by Flipkart, Swiggy, Razorpay, Microsoft.

High growth Emerging
QualificationsBA Psychology / Sociology + UX certification · MA HCI / Cognitive Science
Entrance ExamsCUET · IIT Design (CEED) · Company recruitment
Top InstitutionsIDC IIT Bombay · NID · Srishti Manipal · IIIT Bangalore (HCI)
Eligible StreamsArts · Science · Commerce
Typical salary range (India) 8–25 LPA

⚖️ Civil Services, Law & Public Policy

The most prestigious career destination for humanities students — UPSC, judiciary, policy think-tanks, and diplomacy.

Reading & analysisWritingCurrent affairsLogical reasoningPublic speakingConstitution & polity

⚖️ Civil Services, Law & Public Policy

IAS / IPS / IFS Officer (UPSC)

Leads India's administration, policing and foreign service — the apex civil services.

Stable growth Traditional
QualificationsAny bachelor's degree (Arts/Science/Commerce)
Entrance ExamsUPSC Civil Services Examination (Prelims, Mains, Interview)
Top InstitutionsDelhi University · JNU · Jamia · Allahabad University · Coaching: Vajiram, Vision IAS, Forum IAS
Eligible StreamsArts · Science · Commerce
Typical salary range (India) ₹56,100 starting + perks (~12 LPA CTC), rising to Cabinet Secretary scale

State PSC Officer (Group I / DSP / Tahsildar)

State-level administrative, revenue, police and welfare officer roles via state PSCs.

Stable growth Traditional
QualificationsAny graduate degree
Entrance ExamsTNPSC Group I · KPSC · APPSC · TSPSC · MPSC etc.
Top InstitutionsLoyola College · Madras Christian College · Stella Maris · state universities + coaching
Eligible StreamsArts · Science · Commerce
Typical salary range (India) 8–20 LPA + perks

Lawyer / Corporate Counsel

Advocates in courts, drafts contracts, advises corporates, handles M&A, litigation and constitutional matters.

High growth Traditional
QualificationsBA LLB (5 yr) / LLB (3 yr after graduation) · LLM optional
Entrance ExamsCLAT · AILET · LSAT India · SLAT · state law CETs
Top InstitutionsNLSIU Bangalore · NALSAR Hyderabad · NLU Delhi · Jindal Global Law School · Symbiosis Law
Eligible StreamsArts · Science · Commerce
Typical salary range (India) 6–30 LPA at top corporate firms; ₹3–10 LPA litigation start

Judicial Services Officer (Magistrate / Civil Judge)

Becomes a judge through state Judicial Services Examination.

Stable growth Traditional
QualificationsLLB + Judicial Services Exam
Entrance ExamsState Judicial Services Examination (TN PCS-J, Delhi JS, MP JS etc.)
Top InstitutionsNLUs · Government Law Colleges
Eligible StreamsArts · Science · Commerce
Typical salary range (India) ₹77,840+ (entry Civil Judge) + perks · ~15 LPA CTC

Public Policy Analyst / Think-Tank Researcher

Researches and shapes policy on education, health, climate, gender for ORF, CPR, NITI Aayog, World Bank.

High growth Emerging
QualificationsBA Economics/Pol Sci + MA Public Policy / MPP / MPA
Entrance ExamsCUET PG · NLSIU MPP · TISS PGD-PP
Top InstitutionsNLSIU Bangalore (MPP) · TISS · Ashoka University · IIM Bangalore (MPP) · Jindal School of Govt
Eligible StreamsArts · Commerce · Science
Typical salary range (India) 6–20 LPA

Journalist / News Reporter

Reports for newspapers, news channels and digital platforms — politics, business, sports, investigative.

High growth Emerging

Entrance ExamsNo formal exam — portfolio-driven
Top InstitutionsSelf-learning · MICA Ahmedabad (PG) · Creator-economy bootcamps
Eligible StreamsArts · Science · Commerce

QualificationsBA Mass Comm + MBA Communications / PG Diploma PR

Top InstitutionsMICA Ahmedabad · XIC Mumbai · Symbiosis SCMC · IIMC
Eligible StreamsArts · Commerce · Science

Advertising Copywriter / Brand Strategist

High growth Traditional
QualificationsBA English / Mass Comm + portfolio
Entrance ExamsCUET · MICAT · Agency portfolio round
Top InstitutionsMICA · Miami Ad School Mumbai · IIMC PG Adv & PR
Eligible StreamsArts · Commerce · Science
Typical salary range (India) 4–20 LPA (Creative Directors 25–60 LPA)

Film / Video Editor / Documentary Maker

Edits films, documentaries, OTT series and branded content using Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Avid.

High growth Emerging
QualificationsBA Film & TV / Mass Comm + portfolio · Diploma from FTII/SRFTI
Entrance ExamsFTII / SRFTI entrance (JET) · CUET
Top InstitutionsFTII Pune · SRFTI Kolkata · Whistling Woods Mumbai · IIMC
Eligible StreamsArts · Science · Commerce
Typical salary range (India) 3–18 LPA (freelance project rates vary widely)

🏛️ History, Archaeology & Heritage

Archaeologist (ASI)

Excavates, documents and protects historical sites with the Archaeological Survey of India.

Stable growth Traditional
QualificationsMA History / Archaeology + PG Diploma from Institute of Archaeology, ASI
Entrance ExamsCUET PG · ASI Institute of Archaeology entrance · UPSC (Asst Archaeologist)
Top InstitutionsInstitute of Archaeology (ASI) Delhi · JNU · Deccan College Pune · BHU
Eligible StreamsArts · Science · Commerce
Typical salary range (India) 6–15 LPA (Govt pay-scale + perks)

Museum Curator / Heritage Manager

Curates exhibits and manages collections at museums (National Museum, CSMVS, DAG, Salar Jung, INTACH).

Moderate growth Traditional
QualificationsMA History / Museology / Art History
Entrance ExamsCUET PG · National Museum Institute entrance
Top InstitutionsNational Museum Institute Delhi · MS University Baroda · Calcutta University
Eligible StreamsArts · Science · Commerce
Typical salary range (India) 4–12 LPA

Heritage Tourism Consultant / Cultural Guide

Designs heritage walks, cultural tours and works with state tourism boards and luxury operators.

Moderate growth Emerging
QualificationsBA History / Tourism + Regional Level Guide license (Ministry of Tourism)
Entrance ExamsCUET · IITTM entrance · Ministry of Tourism guide certification
Top InstitutionsIITTM Gwalior · IGNOU · state university tourism programmes
Eligible StreamsArts · Science · Commerce
Typical salary range (India) 3–12 LPA (luxury tour guides earn higher)

Archivist / Records Manager

Manages historical records and digital archives for governments, libraries, corporates.

Stable growth Traditional
QualificationsMA History + PG Diploma in Archives & Records Management (NAI)
Entrance ExamsCUET PG · National Archives of India entrance
Top InstitutionsSchool of Archival Studies (NAI) Delhi · JNU · Delhi University
Eligible StreamsArts · Science · Commerce
Typical salary range (India) 4–10 LPA

Eligible StreamsArts · Science · Commerce

School Teacher / Academic Coordinator (CBSE/ICSE/IB)

Teaches in K-12 schools — top-tier IB and international schools pay competitively.

Stable growth Traditional

College / University Professor (NET-JRF)

Teaches and researches in colleges and universities; assistant professor positions through UGC NET.

Stable growth Traditional
QualificationsMA + UGC NET / JRF · PhD for permanent professorship
Entrance ExamsUGC NET / JRF · CSIR NET (sciences) · PhD entrance (JNU/DU/HCU)
Top InstitutionsJNU · DU · University of Hyderabad · BHU · TISS
Eligible StreamsArts · Science · Commerce
Typical salary range (India) 8–22 LPA (Assistant Professor pay-scale)

Fashion / Textile / Product Designer

Eligible StreamsArts · Science · Commerce

Performing Artist / Music & Dance Professional

Builds career in classical/contemporary music, dance, theatre, playback singing or composition.
