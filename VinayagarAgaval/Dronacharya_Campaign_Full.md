# 📸 AI SPEAKING AVATAR — STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE
# ═══════════════════════════════════════════

## What You Need
1. **Your photo** — Clear, well-lit, front-facing, neutral or slight smile
   - Best: Professional headshot or waist-up photo
   - Lighting: Natural daylight or ring light
   - Background: Clean (office, bookshelf, or plain wall)
   - Resolution: At least 512×512 px

2. **Your script** — From the sections marked with 📸 above

## Method A: Hedra.com (Recommended — Free, No Watermark)

### Steps:
1. Go to **https://www.hedra.com**
2. Sign up free (Google login)
3. Click **"Create"**
4. **Upload your photo** (the clear headshot)
5. **Paste your script** in the text field (use the English or Tamil script from above)
6. **Choose voice** — Select an Indian English voice, OR record your own audio and upload it
7. Click **Generate**
8. Download the video (MP4, no watermark)
9. Import into **CapCut** and place at the end of your main video

### Tips for Hedra:
- Keep each clip under 30 seconds for free tier
- For longer scripts, split into 2 clips and join in CapCut
- Use "Expressive" mode for natural head movement
- Upload your OWN audio recording for most authentic feel

## Method B: CapCut AI Presenter (Free, No Watermark)

### Steps:
1. Download **CapCut Desktop** (free) from capcut.com
2. New project → Import your main video
3. Go to **Text → AI Presenter** (or Smart Tools → Photo Talking)
4. **Upload your photo**
5. **Record audio** — read the script yourself, or use Text-to-Speech
6. CapCut lip-syncs your photo to the audio
7. Place the AI talking clip at the END of the video timeline
8. Export → Full HD → No watermark

## Method C: Record Your Voice + Use SadTalker (100% Free, Open Source)

### Steps:
1. **Record your voice** reading the script on your phone (use Voice Recorder app)
2. Go to **https://huggingface.co/spaces/vinthony/SadTalker** (free web version)
3. Upload your **photo** + your **audio recording**
4. Click Generate → Download video
5. Import into CapCut

## Recommended Audio Recording Tips:
- Quiet room, no echo
- Phone 6 inches from mouth
- Speak slowly and clearly (viewers need to absorb each word)
- Pause slightly between sentences
- Warm, confident, caring tone (you're speaking to worried parents)
- Record in both Tamil and English for different audiences
